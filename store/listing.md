# Google Play listing (copy into Play Console)

**App name** (30 max): SimpleRemote – IR Remote

**Short description** (80 max):
Universal IR remote for AC, TV, fan & projector. For phones with an IR blaster.

**Full description:**

SimpleRemote turns your phone's built-in infrared (IR) blaster into a remote control for your air
conditioner, TV, fan, projector, set-top box, audio system and more.

❄️ Real air-conditioner control
AC remotes send the whole state every time, so SimpleRemote remembers each AC's mode, fan speed,
swing and temperature, and only shows the settings your model actually supports.

🔍 Easy setup
Pick the device type and brand, press "Turn ON / Turn OFF" and say whether it worked. If your brand
isn't listed or nothing works, "Try every code" tests codes from every brand.

📱 Home-screen widget
Power plus the three buttons you use most (AC temperature, TV volume, fan speed) right on your
home screen.

🔒 Private and offline
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your remotes stay on your phone.

Requires a phone with a built-in IR blaster, such as most Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones, and some
Huawei, Honor, Vivo, Realme, TCL and older Samsung, LG and HTC phones. It cannot work on phones
without one.

Brand names are used only to identify compatible devices. SimpleRemote is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any device manufacturer.
